## Taxis

- Colour: "taxi yellow", a warm golden yellow (Dupont M6284, close to Pantone 116). We use `#f5b50c` with a clear coat; it reads
  orange-yellow in shade, never lemon yellow.
- Current models: Toyota Camry Hybrid (most common), Nissan NV200, Ford Escape/Transit Connect, Toyota Sienna/RAV4 hybrids;
  older Crown Victorias are gone from service but still the cliché. **No checker stripe** on modern cabs (that was the
  2000s livery): instead a black circular "T" logo and "NYC" wordmark on the front doors, the medallion number
  (e.g. `3G30`) on the rear quarter and on the roof light, and fare text on the rear doors.
- Roof light: slim box ~0.8 m x 0.25 m x 0.2 m, white/ cream faces with the medallion number, lit when the cab is free
  (newer cabs carry an LED advertising topper instead).
- In traffic, cabs are roughly half of all cars on Midtown avenues.

## Other cars

- Midtown traffic is dominated by black livery SUVs (Suburban, Tahoe, Escalade, Yukon), cabs, mid-size sedans (Camry,
  Accord, Altima), Priuses (rideshare), white vans (Sprinter, Transit, ProMaster) and box trucks.
- Colour mix (US fleet): white ~25%, black ~20%, grey ~18%, silver ~12%, blue ~9%, red ~8%, the rest green/beige/brown.
  Livery SUVs are almost always black. Paint: metallics for silver/grey/blue/red, solids for white/black.
- Real sizes: sedan 4.9 x 1.85 x 1.45 m; Prius 4.54 x 1.76 x 1.49 m; Tahoe 5.13 x 2.0 x 1.89 m; Suburban 5.7 m long;
  Sprinter 5.9 m (short wheelbase).

## MTA bus: New Flyer Xcelsior XD40

- 12.5 m long (41 ft incl. bumpers), 2.59 m wide, ~3.3 m to the top of the roof pods; wheelbase ~7.15 m; front overhang
  ~2.3 m (front door ahead of the front axle), rear door between the axles; low floor ~0.36 m.
- Current NYCT livery (2016+): deep blue body, lighter blue sweep along the lower body rising toward the rear, a thin
  yellow accent near the front, black continuous window band, white roof edge; light-grey roof pods (HVAC over the rear,
  hybrid/battery pod mid-front).
- Front: big one-piece windshield, amber LED destination sign above it ("M15 SOUTH FERRY", "+SBS" routes), bike rack
  folded on the front bumper, bug-eye mirrors on arms, paired round headlights low on the front corners.
- Rear: engine grille louvres, vertical tail light clusters, route number LED sign.

## Box trucks / vans (not modelled yet)

- 16-26 ft box trucks (Isuzu NPR, Hino) with white boxes and roll-up rear doors; FedEx/UPS step vans; white Sprinter
  vans are the most common delivery vehicle (the fleet has a Sprinter).
